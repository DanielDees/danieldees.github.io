    ESCAPE THE BACKROOMS · LEVEL 0

    You weren't paying attention, and you fell through the world. Now you're here: damp carpet, yellowed wallpaper, the endless drone of fluorescent light. Roughly six hundred million square miles of nothing.

    Something else fell through, a long time ago. It is still here. Find what you need, restore the power, and locate the exit elevator — quietly.

    FIELD NOTES

    HOW TO PLAY · DO NOT LOSE

    Everything is done with six keys. Nothing here is timed, and nothing needs quick fingers — this is a game about where you are and how loud you are.

    WASD
    Move. Mouse looks around.
    SHIFT
    Sprint. Fast, drains stamina, and loud — the surest way to be found.
    SPACE
    Jump. Sprinting mid-air drains stamina at half rate.
    C
    Crouch / hide. Slow, but silent, and it fits under things. Hold it by default — Options can make it a toggle.
    E
    Use. Pick things up, press buttons, open panels. Some things are a hold.
    F R
    Lantern on/off · hold to crank it. Only once you have found one.
    ESC
    Pause. These notes and the Options sheet live there. O jumps straight to Options.

    READING THE SCREEN

    • Top left — your objectives, in order. The ▸ lit row is what you are doing now; ✓ dim rows are done. Counters fill as you go.
    • Bottom centre — stamina. It turns red when it fails and blue if you ever find something that makes it bottomless.
    • Bottom centre, smaller — a lantern's remaining charge, when you carry one.
    • H I D D E N — you are crouched and out of sight. While that shows, you are hard to find.
    • Flickering lights — that is not decoration. Lights misbehave near whatever is hunting this floor. Flicker in the distance tells you where it is; flicker around you means it is close.

    Every floor has a different keeper, and they all obey the same five rules. Learn these once and they carry all the way down.

    1. Noise is the whole game. Sprinting is heard from a long way off, walking from a short way, crouching from nowhere at all. Anything you pick up, break or set alight makes a noise at that spot — and something will come and look at it.
    2. Break the line, then stop. Put a wall, a shelf or a rock between you and it, then crouch and stay still. Searching is a timer: it will hunt where it last knew you were, fail, and wander off. Panicking through open ground restarts that timer.
    3. You cannot win a footrace. Stamina runs out before its patience does. Sprint to break contact and reach cover — never to simply outdistance it.
    4. Cover is floor-specific. Every floor gives you one thing it cannot follow you into. Find out what it is early, and know where the nearest one is at all times.
    5. Listen more than you look. Footfalls, breathing, scraping and skittering are all positioned in stereo and fall off with distance. Headphones are worth more than any tactic on this list.

    IF IT CATCHES YOU

    You wake up again. What you keep depends on where you died — the death card tells you plainly. Deaths are counted, never fatal to the run.

    THE BACKROOMS

    Yellow rooms, damp carpet, and one thing that has been down here far longer than you have.

    WHAT TO DO

    1. Collect 3 bottles of almond water.
    2. Find the fuse.
    3. Fit it at the breaker panel to restore power.
    4. Call the exit elevator — it only answers once the power is back.

    THE ENTITY

    It is asleep until you take the first thing. After that it wanders, and it sees a long way down a straight corridor. When it notices you it stops, turns and cries out before it commits — that pause is your head start, so use it to break sight, not to stare. Sometimes it stops to rap on a wall: that is it listening. Every objective you clear makes it faster and more restless, and eventually it starts folding space — crossing a whole stretch of corridor in one step, with a puff of dark fog at each end.

    YOUR COVER

    Distance and corners. Crouched and unseen you are nearly invisible: it will stalk to where it last saw you, search, give up and move on. Once the breaker is fixed your stamina goes bottomless — that is the only reason the last run to the elevator is survivable.

    THE END · THE INFINITE LIBRARY

    One vast room of stacks and reading tables, and a horse-sized spider that pads between them. It is blind.

    WHAT TO DO

    1. Find the floppy disks shelved throughout the room.
    2. Feed every one of them to the terminal on the central desk.

    THE LIBRARIAN

    It hunts by sound, and it keys on motion — standing perfectly still reads as safe even upright, even while you turn to look. Every disk you pull off a shelf is a noise at that shelf, and it comes to check. Take several quickly and it arrives faster and angrier. It does not stay on the floor: when it loses you it climbs the walls and webs across the ceiling to cut the room off, then drops on you from above — a long, telegraphed plunge you survive only by moving out from under it. Crouching does not save you from a drop.

    YOUR COVER

    The reading tables. You fit under them crouched and it cannot reach you there; it will circle, lose interest, and leave. About a minute and a half after your first disk the lights burn down and the room wakes up properly — from then on, plan your route between tables.

    THE NEST · THE CAVE BELOW

    Raw karst lit by blue fungus. You followed something home, and it has children.

    WHAT TO DO

    1. Take the crank lantern from the body beside the dead stair.
    2. Burn all four egg clutches — E held for three seconds, standing still.
    3. Climb the fissure the last burn opens.

    THE LANTERN IS THE LEVEL

    Lit, it physically drives the hatchlings back in every direction — they are photophobic and cannot even hear you through the glow. But its charge runs down, cranking it back up (R) is loud and slows you to a walk, and a flame left burning in open cave is a beacon the matriarch reads fluently. Light is safety from the small ones and a signal to the big one. That trade is the whole game down here.

    THE BROOD & THE MATRIARCH

    Hatchlings hold territories and hunt by sound. One that reaches you latches on: your stamina bleeds and it screams your position to its mother until you shake it off (swing the mouse hard) or burn it off with the lantern. The matriarch tends her clutches on a patrol. Silk-laced ground near a nest carries your footfalls to her at twice the range; the stream hides them; scree gives you away. Every burn sets off a frenzy, kills the local fungus-light, and drops a rockfall that reshapes the tunnels.

    YOUR COVER

    The squeezes — tight crawls you pass crouched and she cannot follow you through at all. They are this floor's reading tables. The stream is worth wading for the silence; the chasm will kill you on its own if you fall in.
