## Description

The stair below the library does not end so much as give up. Cut stone degrades into natural
rock, the treads lose their edges, and the shaft opens through the ceiling of a cavern vault.
The blue glow every descender reports resolves, at last, into its source: veins of
bioluminescent fungus threading the rock, brightest where water seeps. It is the only light
this level gives you for free.

Wanderers who have seen the famous cave systems of Level 8 call this place its permutation —
Level 8 turned wrong side out, a karst network of crawl-tunnels, chimney squeezes, and
cathedral chambers forested with stalagmites. A black stream runs through the low galleries.
There is a rock bridge over a drop the fungus-light does not reach the bottom of. Footing is
the terrain here: scree slopes that roar underfoot, squeezes you take on your knees, water
you wade blind.

And everywhere — thin at the cave mouths, thickening as you go wrong — silk.

Threads first. Then sheets on the walls, hammocks sagging from the ceilings, wrapped bundles
of a size you will decide not to think about. There is no map of THE NEST and no need for one.
The web is the map. It thickens toward what it protects. You navigate this level by reading
how afraid you should be.

Behind you, the last flight of the library stair is sheathed in fresh silk. Something came
down this way recently, in a hurry, and it webbed the door behind it.

## Entities

**The Librarian.** It's here. Of course it's here — you watched it dig, and you followed it
home. But home changes it. In the library it was a keeper in its keeping; down here it is a
*parent*. It circulates between the brood chambers on a tending patrol, and near the nests the
ground itself is laced with silk: it feels footfalls through the web the way a fisherman feels
the line. Out in the open cave it is duller than you remember — slower to commit, reliant on
sound carried to it. Near the eggs, it is faster than you have ever seen it.

**Hatchlings.** A handful — five, six, no account agrees — cat-sized, pale, each wearing
its mother's eight eyes and using none of them, each patrolling its own territory in the
dark. They hunt by sound and they fear light: a strong
beam physically drives them back, hissing, at its edge. Alone, one cannot kill you. That is
not its job. One that reaches you *latches* — and screams, a continuous carrying shriek that
tells the parent exactly where its child is and what its child has found. They are not the
death in this level. They are the alarm system, and the alarm has legs.

## Survival

- **The lantern is your life. Manage it like one.** The hand-crank lantern (take it; its last
  owner is past minding) repels the hatchlings — but the charge runs down, cranking it is a
  loud ratcheting grind that carries down every tunnel, and a light held burning in open cave
  is a beacon the Librarian reads fluently. Burn light to hold the small ones off. Kill it to
  hide from the big one. Crank where the cave is already loud.
- **The stream is the one place to hurry.** Running water masks your noise. Wade it fast;
  it is the safest loud thing in the level.
- **Crouch discipline carries over.** Everything you learned on the library carpet is true on
  stone — except near the nests, where the silk-laced ground roughly doubles how far your
  steps carry. Read the web density and slow down accordingly.
- **If one latches: burn it off or shake it off, immediately, and then move** — not away from
  where you are, away from where you *were heard*. The parent is already coming.
- **Fire is shelter, briefly.** A burning clutch is the one light the brood will not cross.
  Stand in the glow while it lasts.

## Exits

The way you came is sealed — webbed shut by the thing you followed. The databases have nothing
on THE NEST; as far as the record is concerned you stopped existing at the top of the stair.

The level itself offers one bargain, and it is written in silk and instinct rather than words:
the brood chambers. Burn them — all of them — and the cave will convulse; wanderers'-instinct
says every rockfall reshapes the maze, closing old throats and cracking new ones. Somewhere in
the last of those convulsions, accounts insist, a fissure opens: a climbable chimney breathing
cold air that smells — impossibly, after everything — like *outside*, or at least like
somewhere that is not this.

Nothing that has lost its whole brood stops hunting you. The climb is not a victory lap.

—found the stream. blessed stream. it can't hear anything over the water, i'm sure of it now.
i crank the lantern by the falls and i wade the low gallery like i'm running late for work,

—the ground near the eggs is strung. i put a hand flat on the rock and felt my own heartbeat

—burned the first clutch today. three seconds with the striker, longest of my life, sparks

so listen: light for the children, dark for the mother, water for the noise, knees for the
silk. burn all of them. burn every last

## Design Notes

Dev-facing; the lore parser ignores this section. This is the locked v3.0.0 design.

### Concept

Hatchery × canon Level 8 (The Cave Systems). Rocky cavern level; webs are cosmetic decoration
whose density signals nest proximity; the Librarian itself is the apex threat (no new big
spider — full reuse of the spider AI/gait/mesh); only a handful of hatchlings.

### Arrival

Spiral stair degrades into natural rock and opens through a cavern ceiling; the last flight is
webbed shut behind the player (no return — mirrors the crashed elevator in THE END). Blue glow
continuity: the shaft's glow was fungus-light all along.

### Terrain

- Karst network: crawl-squeezes (reuse crouch/`underTable`-style clearance), large stalagmite
  chambers, a black underground stream, loud scree patches, one rock bridge over a void.
- **Stream**: wading masks the player's noise — the designated safe-fast zone (inversion of
  "water is loud"; here running water is cover).
- **Scree**: extra-loud surface (surface gain on the existing hearing model).
- Web sheets/hammocks/bundles: cosmetic, density-graded toward brood chambers = diegetic
  wayfinding. Lantern beam catching web sheets should look beautiful and feel suicidal.

### Lantern (core resource)

Found on a corpse near spawn, with a striker (the same corpse carries the found-document
journal). Beam repels hatchlings (cone test, they recoil at its edge); charge drains in use;
cranking recharges but emits loud ratcheting (hearing event); sustained light draws the
Librarian (glow-seek steering while lit beyond a threshold time).

### Hatchlings (5–6)

Territorial, sound-hunting, photophobic (the eight eyes are why the light hurts, not how
they find you). Cheap steering (no BFS needed — territory
leash + sound pursuit). On reach: latch — screen shake, stamina drain, continuous screech that
beacons the Librarian (feeds its `lastKnown` every frame while latched). Removed by beam-burn
or shake-off (mash/rapid-turn). Not individually lethal; they are the alarm system.

### The Librarian, at home

Same AI core. Tending patrol between brood chambers instead of browse-wander. Near nests, the
silk-laced ground doubles the player's effective noise radius; in open cave its reaction is
duller (longer decide time, smaller radii). Crouch-silent rules carry over from THE END.
Latched-hatchling screech and sustained lantern light are its two long-range senses.

### Objective loop — burn 4 egg clutches

1. **Find** the clutch via web density, managing lantern vs the local hatchling.
2. **Ignite**: held interaction ~3s (stationary, striker sparking — self-lit and
   web-grounded: maximum vulnerability by design).
3. **Frenzy**: level-wide event ~60–90s — Librarian comes at run speed, all hatchlings
   frenzy; the burning clutch is a temporary safe light (brood won't cross the burn glow).
4. **The cave answers**: staged rockfall closes one route and opens another (staged level
   mutation, like the fold/blackout patterns), and the fungus veins in that region dim —
   the level gets darker as you win.

### Escape

After clutch 4: the Librarian hunts permanently (endgame mode, as the library's post-terminal
state). Final rockfall opens a climbable fissure chimney — real geometry, walkable/jumpable
like the v2.6 stairs — ascended with the Librarian coming up the cave behind. Fade on the
updraft. Win / Level 3 TBD.

### Audio

Dripwater percussion with long echo repeats (extend the `sfxStoneStep` echo pattern), stream
white-noise masking bed, hatchling skitters tuned to be indistinguishable from drips at
distance, fungus-glow breathing (reuse the hole-glow pulse), lantern crank ratchet, latch
screech, clutch fire.
